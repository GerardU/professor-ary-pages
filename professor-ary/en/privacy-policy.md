# Privacy Policy for Professor Ary

Effective date: August 12, 2026

Professor Ary is designed for caregiver-led flashcard practice. The app does not require an account and does not ask for personal information.

## Data Collection

Professor Ary does not ask for names, email addresses, accounts, advertising identifiers, or information about a child. It does not sell data or use it for advertising.

Settings, favorites, selected card sets, and session progress are stored locally on the device so the app can remember your routine. That data is not sent to the developer.

## Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. To verify and manage Plus, RevenueCat processes a random anonymous app user ID generated by its SDK, device type and operating system, the Apple receipt, and transaction and entitlement status. We do not give RevenueCat account or child data, and we do not see or store payment details. Legacy v1.0 access is granted only after a verified StoreKit AppTransaction, not from local progress.

## Analytics

Professor Ary does not use a third-party analytics SDK. When a release configures the optional first-party collector, it receives only allowlisted aggregate events with app version, locale, module, set, mode, access tier, surface, trigger, plan, and experiment variant. It receives no account, device, child, advertising, location, date-of-birth, IP/User-Agent, or free-form text data. If no endpoint is configured, analytics is disabled.

## Network Use

The core flashcard experience works offline with bundled images and audio. Network access is used only for purchases, entitlement checks, and the optional first-party aggregate collector described above.

## Children

Professor Ary is intended for use by parents, guardians, and caregivers with children. The app includes no user accounts, no advertising, no tracking of children, no social features, and no user-generated content. External links and purchase surfaces sit behind a grown-up gate or the App Store purchase flow.

## Contact

For support or privacy questions, use the support contact listed on the app store page.
