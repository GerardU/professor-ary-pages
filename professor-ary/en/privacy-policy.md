# Privacy Policy for Professor Ary

Effective date: August 3, 2026

Professor Ary is designed for caregiver-led flashcard practice. The app does not require an account and does not ask for personal information.

## Data Collection

Professor Ary does not collect, sell, or share personal data.

Settings, favorites, selected card sets, and session progress are stored locally on the device so the app can remember your routine. That data is not sent to the developer.

## Purchases

Professor Ary offers an optional Plus plan (subscriptions and a one-time lifetime option). Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store; we use RevenueCat to verify entitlements. We never see or store your payment details, and purchase status is linked only to an anonymous app user id.

## Analytics

The app sends anonymized usage signals (for example: a guided round was completed) to TelemetryDeck. Signals contain no personal data, no identifiers for children, no precise location, and no advertising identifiers. Analytics is used only to improve the learning experience.

## Network Use

The core flashcard experience works offline with bundled images and audio. Network access is used only for purchases, entitlement checks, and the anonymized analytics described above.

External support or privacy links open outside the app behind a grown-up gate and are handled by the relevant browser.

## Children

Professor Ary is intended for use by parents, guardians, and caregivers with children. The app includes no user accounts, no advertising, no tracking of children, no social features, and no user-generated content. External links and purchase surfaces sit behind a grown-up gate or the App Store purchase flow.

## Contact

For support or privacy questions, use the support contact listed on the app store page.
