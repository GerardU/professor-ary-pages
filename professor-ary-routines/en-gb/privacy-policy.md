# Privacy Policy for Professor Ary: Visual Routine

Effective date: August 29, 2026

Professor Ary: Visual Routine is a caregiver-led visual routine app. The free experience works offline without an account. Cloud backup and caregiver sharing are optional Routines+ features that an adult must choose.

## Data stored on your device

The app may store child profiles, routine and step text, schedules, reminders, routine history, preferences, custom photos, and voice cues in private app storage and a local database. This information stays on the device unless an adult enables Routines+ cloud sync or deliberately exports or shares a routine.

Widgets, Live Activities, and notifications receive only the minimum state needed to work. Lock-screen wording is neutral by default and does not include a child's name or custom routine text.

## Optional Routines+ cloud data

If an adult enables cloud features, Supabase processes the Sign in with Apple account identifier and, when Apple supplies it, the adult's email or private-relay address. The service also stores the household membership and the routine, schedule, history, and custom-media data selected for sync. Access is limited to the household owner and invited caregivers.

Custom photos and voice cues use private storage and temporary signed access. They are not published at a permanent public URL. Routines cloud data is isolated from the other Professor Ary products even though the services may share the same infrastructure provider.

## Purchases

Apple processes subscriptions and the lifetime purchase through the App Store. RevenueCat processes an app user identifier, Apple transaction and receipt information, product identifiers, and entitlement status so the app can grant and restore Routines+. We do not receive or store payment-card details.

## Sharing, export, and caregivers

An adult may export a board or create a routine share link. The default shared snapshot excludes child and household information, routine history, notes, custom photos and audio, authentication data, and internal identifiers. A share can expire or be revoked. Imports are previewed and validated before they change local data.

Caregiver invitations use a single-purpose token with an expiry. Only an adult may create, accept, revoke, or manage household access.

## Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The app contains no advertising SDK and does not use data for advertising, cross-app tracking, behavioural profiling, or child analytics. It does not request App Tracking Transparency permission.

At launch, optional product analytics has no configured production destination. Child names, routine or step text, schedules, history, photos, audio, household details, Apple identity, email, and raw internal identifiers are never permitted analytics fields. Supabase and other service providers may process limited network and security information to deliver and protect their services.

## Retention and deletion

Local information remains until an adult deletes it in the app or removes the app and its data from the device. Cloud information remains while the Routines cloud account is active or as needed to provide requested sharing and synchronization.

Settings offers separate controls to export data, delete local data, and delete the Routines cloud account. Cloud deletion removes Routines household access, routines, runs, private media, shares, invitations, device records, and entitlement cache from the live Routines service. A shared provider identity may remain only when another Professor Ary product still uses it; this does not preserve Routines data or access. Apple purchase history remains with Apple so an eligible purchase can be restored later. Removing an account does not cancel an App Store subscription.

Expired or revoked links and invitations stop granting access. Minimal security and deletion records may be retained only as required to prevent replay, finish deletion safely, meet legal obligations, or age out of provider backups.

## Children and adult controls

The app is designed for adults to prepare and supervise routines for children. Purchases, accounts, external links, caregiver management, sharing, export off the device, and deletion are behind an adult check. The app has no child-facing social features, advertising, or public profiles.

## Service providers and legal requests

Apple provides App Store purchases and Sign in with Apple. RevenueCat verifies purchase entitlement. Supabase provides optional authentication, cloud database, private storage, and server functions. These providers process data under their own security and privacy commitments. We may disclose information if legally required or to protect users and the service, and we do not sell personal information.

## Your choices

You can use the free local experience without an account, decline optional permissions, use neutral notification text, avoid cloud and sharing, revoke shares or caregivers, export your data, and delete local or cloud data. You can manage subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.
