# Visual Routine Subscriptions and Purchases

Professor Ary: Visual Routine has a useful free experience and three optional Routines+ purchase choices. The App Store screen in the app is the authoritative source for your local price, tax, trial eligibility, and billing terms.

## Purchase choices

- **Monthly**: an automatically renewing one-month subscription with no introductory trial.
- **Annual**: an automatically renewing one-year subscription. Eligible accounts may see a two-week introductory free trial.
- **Lifetime**: a one-time, non-consumable purchase that unlocks the supported Routines+ entitlement without a recurring subscription.

Routines+ adds multiple children, more routines and steps within safe limits, the complete visual library, more custom media, full visible history, optional cloud backup and multi-device sync, caregiver access, premium widgets, and footer-free export. Free continues to work locally without an account or ads.

## Trial and renewal

Apple decides trial eligibility. If the App Store purchase sheet offers a trial, it begins when you confirm the purchase and converts to the displayed annual price unless you cancel before the trial ends. A subscription renews automatically unless canceled through Apple at least 24 hours before the current period ends, subject to Apple's current rules. The app does not infer eligibility or hard-code a price.

## Restore purchases

Open **Settings → Routines+ → Restore Purchases** while using the same Apple Account that made the purchase. If you use optional cloud features, sign in first so the restored RevenueCat entitlement and the Routines account use the same identity. A restore should not charge you again.

If restore does not work, confirm network access, the Apple Account, and that the purchase appears in Apple's purchase history. Then email [professoraryapp@gmail.com](mailto:professoraryapp@gmail.com) with the app version and the product type. Do not send receipts containing personal information unless support specifically requests a privacy-safe item.

## Manage or cancel

On iPhone or iPad, open **Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Professor Ary: Visual Routine**. Cancellation stops a subscription from renewing; access normally continues until the paid period ends. Apple handles billing and refund decisions.

Deleting the app, local data, or a Routines cloud account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel through Apple separately. A lifetime purchase has no renewal to cancel.

## Expiry and offline use

The app periodically asks Apple and RevenueCat to refresh entitlement status. A previously verified entitlement may remain available for a limited offline period, then refresh when the device reconnects. If a subscription expires or is refunded, Routines+ cloud writes and premium views stop safely; local Free data and the core routine experience remain available within Free limits. The app does not delete older local canonical records solely because access changes.
