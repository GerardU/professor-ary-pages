# Professor Ary: Visual Routine Support

Create calm picture routines, show Now and Next, and make time visible. The core app works offline without an account or ads.

## Get started

1. Open **Routines** and choose a template or create a routine.
2. Add, reorder, or edit the visual steps.
3. Choose **Start**. The child view shows the current and next step.
4. Use the grown-up control to pause, add or subtract a minute, end a timer early, skip a step, or end the routine.

You can also open **First/Then** for a quick two-step board.

## Common questions

### Does the app need an account or internet connection?

No. Free routines, timers, schedules, notifications, and recent history work locally without an account. An internet connection is needed only for purchases, optional Routines+ cloud features, caregiver invitations, and server-backed share links.

### What is included in Free?

Free includes one child, two active routines with up to eight steps each, core templates and visual cues, First/Then, timers, local schedules and notifications, three days of visible history, limited custom photo and voice cues, and branded image/PDF export. Older local history stays stored even when it is not visible in Free.

### What is included in Routines+?

Routines+ adds multiple children, unlimited active routines and steps within safe device and storage limits, the complete visual library, more custom media, full visible history, optional cloud backup and multi-device sync, caregiver access, premium widgets, and footer-free export.

### How do I restore a purchase?

Open **Settings → Routines+ → Restore Purchases** while signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase. See [subscription and purchase help](./subscription) for trial, renewal, cancellation, and lifetime details.

### How do timers stay accurate?

Timers use saved timestamps and pause intervals, not only an on-screen countdown. If the app closes or the device clock changes, reopen the routine and review the recovered timer. A grown-up can pause, resume, adjust by one minute, or end it early.

### Why did a reminder not appear?

Check **Settings → Notifications** in the app and the device's notification settings. Focus modes and system scheduling can delay or suppress an alert. The app uses neutral lock-screen wording by default.

### How do I add a caregiver?

With Routines+ and cloud enabled, open **Settings → Account and family**, create an invitation, and share it with the intended adult. Invitations expire and can be revoked. The owner can remove a caregiver at any time.

### What does a routine share include?

The preview shows exactly what will be shared. By default it contains only a sanitized routine snapshot and excludes children, household details, history, notes, private photos and audio, and internal identifiers. You can revoke the link.

### How do I delete data?

Use **Settings → Privacy and data**. Local deletion, cloud-account deletion, and App Store subscription management are separate actions. Read the [account deletion guide](./account-deletion) before deleting cloud data.

### Is this a behaviour or medical treatment tool?

No. Visual Routine is an organization and communication aid for caregiver-led use. It does not diagnose, treat, guarantee outcomes, replace supervision, or provide emergency alerts.

## Accessibility

The app supports text alternatives for visual cues, VoiceOver labels, Dynamic Type, increased contrast, reduced motion, and non-audio completion cues. If a screen is difficult to use with an assistive technology, please tell us the device, iOS version, app version, and screen involved. Do not send child names, photos, voice recordings, or routine details.
