# Delete Your Visual Routine Data or Cloud Account

Local data, the optional Routines cloud account, and your App Store subscription are separate. Choose the action that matches what you want to remove.

## Delete local data only

In the app, open **Settings → Privacy and data → Delete local data**, pass the grown-up check, review the scope, and confirm. This removes Visual Routine data from that device. It does not delete cloud copies on other devices, revoke an App Store purchase, or cancel a subscription.

## Delete the Routines cloud account

1. Open **Settings → Account and family** while signed in.
2. Export anything you want to keep.
3. Choose **Delete Routines Cloud Account**.
4. Pass the grown-up check and review the consequences.
5. Confirm the deletion request and keep the app open until it reports completion.

The live Routines service removes your Routines household access, routines, steps, schedules, run history, custom media, shares, invitations, registered devices, and cached Routines+ entitlement. Active links and invitations stop working. Local data on a device is removed only if you select that option; otherwise it remains local and no longer syncs.

If the same Sign in with Apple identity is still used by another Professor Ary product, the shared authentication row may remain so that the sibling product is not damaged. This does not preserve a Visual Routine account, content, entitlement cache, or household access.

Deletion cannot erase Apple's purchase record. You can restore an eligible purchase if you create a Routines account again. Provider security records and backups, if any, age out under their limited operational retention and are not used to reactivate the deleted account.

## Cancel a subscription separately

Deleting data or an account does not cancel billing. On iPhone or iPad, open **Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Professor Ary: Visual Routine**, then cancel the subscription. Apple controls billing, renewal, and refunds.

## If you cannot access the app

Email [professoraryapp@gmail.com](mailto:professoraryapp@gmail.com) from the address associated with Sign in with Apple, or provide the private-relay address shown for the app. State that you want to delete the **Visual Routine cloud account**. Do not email child names, routine text, photos, audio, invitation links, or share tokens. We may ask for a privacy-safe verification step before acting.
