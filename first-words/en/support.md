# First Words Support

For help with First Words, use the support contact listed on the app store page.

## Common Questions

### Does the app require an account?

No. First Words works without account creation.

### Does the app include ads or in-app purchases?

No. First Words is a standalone app with no ads and no in-app purchases.

### Can the cards be used offline?

Yes. Card images and English audio are bundled with the app.

### Where is progress stored?

Settings, favorites, selected card sets, and session progress are stored locally on the device.

### Can I print the cards?

Yes. Open Print & PDF, choose a source, adjust the layout, and generate a printable sheet.
