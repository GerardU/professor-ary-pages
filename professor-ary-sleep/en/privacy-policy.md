# Privacy Policy for Professor Ary: Baby Sleep

Effective date: August 19, 2026

Professor Ary: Baby Sleep is designed with privacy and calm in mind. The free tier works entirely on your device without an account.

## Data Collection & Storage
- **Free Tier**: Babies, sleep sessions, reminders, and settings are stored locally on your device in a private SQLite database. No sleep data is transmitted to our servers.
- **Sleep+ Cloud**: When you subscribe to Sleep+ and choose to enable cloud backup, your baby profiles and sleep sessions are securely stored in Supabase using Sign in with Apple authentication. We do not require or collect child photos, contacts, location, or health records.

## In-App Purchases
Subscriptions and purchases are processed directly by Apple through the App Store. RevenueCat processes anonymous app user identifiers and Apple transaction receipts to verify entitlement status. We never see or store your payment details.

## Analytics & Tracking
Professor Ary: Baby Sleep contains no third-party analytics SDKs, no advertising trackers, and no data brokers. We do not sell or monetize personal data.

## Account Deletion
You can delete your cloud account and all associated household cloud data at any time in Settings → Account. Account deletion is permanent and separate from App Store subscription management.

## Contact
For privacy questions or support inquiries, contact us at: https://gerardu.github.io/professor-ary-pages/professor-ary-sleep/en/support
