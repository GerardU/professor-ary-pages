# Professor Ary: Baby Sleep Support

Calm, focused wake-window planning and nap tracking for early parenthood.

## Common Questions

### Does the app require an account?

No. Professor Ary: Baby Sleep works completely offline without an account on the free tier.

### How are wake windows calculated?

Predictions start from published age-appropriate population patterns and gradually adjust to your baby's recent sleep patterns as you log more days. Predictions are calm planning estimates, not medical rules.

### What is included with Sleep+?

Sleep+ includes cloud backup, multi-device synchronization, sharing with one caregiver, custom reminder timing, quiet hours, unlimited history, insights, and interactive widgets.

### How do I invite a caregiver?

With Sleep+ active, go to Settings → Family to generate an invite code or link for one additional caregiver.

### How do I delete my cloud data?

Navigate to Settings → Account → Delete cloud account. You can choose to keep local logs on your device or remove everything.
